Etchling — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

I&M Games ("we", "us") develops Etchling, a daily picture-logic puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information Etchling and the third-party services it uses collect, why, and what choices you have. It covers this app only, not any website or other product.

The short version

Etchling has no player-facing account, login, chat or user-generated content. Puzzle progress, streak and settings are saved in the app's device storage. The app shows ads through Google AdMob and LevelPlay, uses consent-gated Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, and uses Unity In-App Purchasing to connect to Google Play. Ads and Firebase follow the applicable privacy choices in Google's consent form. Unity's required purchase/service data is described separately below; declining Firebase analytics does not turn off that purchase-service processing. You can review applicable privacy choices in Settings.

Gameplay progress and device storage

Your daily streak, completed puzzles and packs, sound/haptics preferences and completion calendar are saved in the app's private device storage. They are not tied to your name or email and are not sent to a studio progress server. Clearing app storage removes the local copy. Android's system backup/restore may restore app data on reinstall, depending on your device and backup settings; we do not operate that backup or guarantee recovery.

Advertising

Etchling shows ads through Google AdMob, mediated by the LevelPlay (ironSource) platform, which in turn can route a given ad request to one of several third-party ad networks. Showing an ad can involve your device's advertising identifier (e.g. Android's Advertising ID) and general technical information (device type, approximate location derived from IP, app version) being shared with AdMob, LevelPlay, and the network that ultimately fills the ad, for the purpose of selecting and measuring that ad. Etchling never sells your data itself, but these advertising partners operate under their own privacy policies and may combine what they receive from Etchling with information from other apps or sources:

- Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy

- ironSource / LevelPlay (Unity/AppLovin): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

The ads you may see are a small banner at the bottom of the screen, an occasional full-screen ad between puzzles (never while you are solving one), and optional rewarded videos you choose to watch — for an extra Check, to earn a Reveal, to unlock a puzzle pack early, or to replay a past day's daily puzzle. Each rewarded video asks you to confirm before it plays. Etchling never requires an ad to be watched to keep playing the core daily puzzle.

Purchases and Restore

Remove ads is an optional one-time purchase processed by Google Play under Google's terms and privacy policy. Etchling does not receive your payment-card or bank-account details. The app uses Google Play purchase records to grant and restore the ad-free entitlement, and caches that entitlement on your device. Uninstalling Etchling removes its local cache but does not cancel the purchase held by Google Play; use the same Google Play account to recover it. We do not operate a separate server for purchase validation.

- Google Play: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Etchling uses Unity In-App Purchasing 5.4 to communicate with Google Play. The SDK processes purchase/service records such as the product, transaction identifier, purchase outcome, price/currency, installation and session identifiers, technical device/app information and country. It may create an anonymous service identity without asking you to register. These records support purchases, recovery, service operation and diagnostics; some transaction records are also processed by Unity for security, reconciliation and its financial/legal obligations. This processing is separate from Etchling's optional Firebase purchase events. Etchling denies optional Unity advertising/analytics linking, including linking Android advertising or Firebase identifiers through this SDK. That denial does not disable required purchase/service reporting. We do not enable Unity webshop or direct-to-consumer payment features, or ask you for an email address for purchasing.

- Unity: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy

Analytics

Etchling uses Firebase Analytics (a Google service) to record in-app events — for example, that a puzzle was started or completed, how long it took, whether a streak continued, or that a setting was opened — so we can tell whether the game is working and enjoyable. These events do not include your name, email, or any text you type, because the app never asks for any. Analytics only runs when your privacy choices allow it (see below). Firebase Analytics can involve a device identifier and general technical/usage information being sent to Google; see Google's own policy for how Firebase handles that data:

Purchase analytics follows the same privacy choices. When analytics is enabled, Firebase can receive events about the Remove ads offer, purchase attempts, completed purchases, failures and Restore, including the product identifier and result or failure category. These events help us understand and improve the purchase flow. Our custom events do not include store receipts, purchase tokens or payment-card details. Firebase's own purchase measurement can include product information and price, as described in Google's disclosure.

- Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Crash reports

Etchling uses Firebase Crashlytics (a Google service) so we can find and fix crashes and errors. If the app crashes or hits an unexpected error, a report is sent to Google containing technical details: what the app was doing when it happened (for example, which screen was open), the device model, Android version, app version, and a random identifier created for this installation of the app. Crash reports contain no name, email, or anything you type, and are never used for advertising. Like analytics, crash reporting only runs when your privacy choices allow it (see below). Firebase's privacy information above covers Crashlytics too.

Your privacy choices

Where the law requires it — for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland — Etchling shows Google's consent form the first time you open the app, and again whenever it is needed. Ads and analytics follow what you choose there. If you do not consent to information being stored on and read from your device, Firebase Analytics and crash reporting stay off and any ads shown are not personalized; you can still play the full game. If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law, the same Google form lets you opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information. You can review or change your choices at any time from Settings, under Privacy Options (shown whenever a choice applies to you); a change takes effect straight away.

Children

Etchling is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information in a way this policy doesn't account for, contact us using the address below and we will address it.

Data retention and deletion

On-device data (progress, streak, settings) is retained until you uninstall the app or clear its storage — entirely under your control. Analytics event data sent to Firebase is retained according to Google's own Firebase Analytics data retention settings for this project, and crash reports according to Firebase Crashlytics' own retention period. We do not maintain a separate server or database of player data outside these third-party services.

Unity purchase/service records follow Unity's applicable retention practices; transaction and service records are separate from your local gameplay files. Contact us to request access/deletion for records Unity processes on our behalf. For records Unity processes as an independent controller, its policy explains your rights and request route. Declining analytics or uninstalling does not itself delete records already held by service providers.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, and to object to or restrict processing. Contact us at the address below for requests concerning Etchling and data our providers process on our behalf; we will help route the request to the relevant service. Providers' linked policies also describe rights concerning data they control independently. You can withdraw applicable consent in Settings and clear local app storage, but these actions do not themselves erase previously transmitted service records or cancel a Google Play purchase.

Permissions

Etchling requests only the permissions it needs to connect to the internet, show ads (including access to your device's advertising ID, which you can reset or delete in your device's settings) and vibrate for haptic feedback. The advertising and analytics components it includes also add a few standard permissions Android grants without asking you (for example, checking the network connection). Etchling never asks for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, calls or files.

Changes to this policy

If what Etchling collects or how it's used changes, this text will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
