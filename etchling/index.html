Etchling — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-22

I&M Games ("we", "us") develops Etchling, a daily picture-logic puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information Etchling and the third-party services it uses collect, why, and what choices you have. It covers this app only, not any website or other product.

The short version

Etchling has no account, no login, and no chat or user-generated content. Your puzzle progress, streak, and settings are stored only on your device. The app shows ads (through Google AdMob and the LevelPlay mediation network) and uses Firebase Analytics to understand how the game is used; both are gated behind the consent choice you make the first time you open the app, and both can involve device/advertising identifiers being shared with those providers under their own privacy policies.

Information stored only on your device

Your daily streak, which puzzles and packs you've completed, your sound/haptics preferences, and your completion calendar are saved locally on your device (in the app's private storage area) so your progress survives closing and reopening the app. This data is not an account, is not tied to your name or email, and is not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it permanently and cannot be recovered — we have no copy of it.

Advertising

Etchling shows ads through Google AdMob, mediated by the LevelPlay (ironSource) platform, which in turn can route a given ad request to one of several third-party ad networks. Showing an ad can involve your device's advertising identifier (e.g. Android's Advertising ID) and general technical information (device type, approximate location derived from IP, app version) being shared with AdMob, LevelPlay, and the network that ultimately fills the ad, for the purpose of selecting and measuring that ad. Etchling never sells your data itself, but these advertising partners operate under their own privacy policies and may combine what they receive from Etchling with information from other apps or sources:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • ironSource / LevelPlay (Unity/AppLovin): https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

Rewarded ads (used for extra Checks, Reveal a line, and unlocking a puzzle pack) and the interstitial shown between puzzles work the same way. Etchling never requires an ad to be watched to keep playing the core daily puzzle — assist ads are always optional.

Analytics

Etchling uses Firebase Analytics (a Google service) to record in-app events — for example, that a puzzle was started or completed, how long it took, whether a streak continued, or that a setting was opened — so we can tell whether the game is working and enjoyable. These events do not include your name, email, or any text you type, because the app never asks for any. Firebase Analytics can involve a device identifier and general technical/usage information being sent to Google; see Google's own policy for how Firebase handles that data:

  • Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Your consent choice

The first time you open Etchling, you're asked whether to allow personalized ads and analytics. Choosing "Don't allow" still lets you play the full game; ads may be shown but less tailored to you, and analytics collection stays off. This choice is saved on your device and applied every time the app starts. At this time, changing your answer requires clearing the app's storage or reinstalling it (there is no in-app "change my answer" screen yet) — treat that as clearing your choice back to unanswered, which the app will ask again on next launch.

Children

Etchling is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information in a way this policy doesn't account for, contact us using the address below and we will address it.

Data retention and deletion

On-device data (progress, streak, settings) is retained until you uninstall the app or clear its storage — entirely under your control. Analytics event data sent to Firebase is retained according to Google's own Firebase Analytics data retention settings for this project. We do not maintain a separate server or database of player data outside these third-party services.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or similar regional laws). Because Etchling itself stores no personal data on any server we control, the practical way to exercise these rights today is: (1) decline personalized ads/analytics at the consent prompt, and (2) uninstall the app or clear its storage to remove all on-device data. For anything related to data already sent to Google/Firebase/AdMob/LevelPlay, those providers' own privacy policies (linked above) describe how to make requests directly to them.

Permissions

Etchling requests only the device permissions needed to show ads and play haptic feedback. It does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage access beyond its own private app folder.

Changes to this policy

If what Etchling collects or how it's used changes, this text will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
