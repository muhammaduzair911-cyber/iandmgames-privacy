Jiggly Jam Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026. Published by I&M Games.

Jiggly Jam is a puzzle game for Android. This page explains what the game does with information. The short version: the version covered here collects no personal information and sends nothing off your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not ask you to sign in, does not ask for your name, email, age or location, and does not access your contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress (levels finished, stars, helpers), your settings (sound, music, vibration, reduced motion, high-contrast symbols) and small game states, such as when your daily gift was last claimed. This is stored only on your device. We cannot see it. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, it is deleted.

Permissions

The game asks for the Vibrate permission only, to give small buzzes when you play (you can switch this off in Settings). It does not have internet permission in this version.

Ads, purchases and analytics

This version has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics and no crash reporting. If a later version adds any of these, we will update this policy before that version is released, and say what is collected, why, and how to choose. Ads in a later version will follow the Google Play Families policy, and will be child-safe where players are children.

Children

The game is made to be friendly for the whole family. Because it collects no personal information, it does not collect personal information from children.

Sharing

We do not share, sell or send any information to anyone, because we do not collect any.

Your choices

You can delete everything the game has stored by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling it.

Changes

If this policy changes we will change the date above. Material changes will be shown in the game's store page and release notes.
